# Active & Passive Voice source audit

The 20 records in `active-passive.js` were checked on 26 September 2026 against the named exam, date/shift, four options, and explicit answer on each linked secondary record. They match the 3/3/3/2/2/3/2/2 module blueprint, include both directions, and contain six easy, ten medium and four hard items. Difficulty is editorial judgment.

The repository owner approved the reviewed set for live use in Codex on 26 September 2026: “Approve this reviewed set for live use.” The records are **verified for publication based on secondary exam records** and the approver and timestamp are stored with every question. Official final-key confirmation has not been obtained.

Original exam wording belongs to SSC. The [SSC website reproduction policy](https://ssc.nic.in/Portal/WebsitePolicy) permits accurate reproduction with prominent source acknowledgment, except third-party copyrighted material. The app attributes every question to its exam and source record. Third-party explanatory prose is not copied; the short explanations in this bank are editorial summaries.

## Checks and limitations

- Question prompts and option ordering follow each individual source record. Only line breaks and incidental whitespace were normalized. Distractor errors such as “baken” are preserved.
- Answer letters are transcribed from published keys. Scoring never asks an AI to determine correctness.
- Exam year follows the named examination cycle; the actual sitting date is also stored. Some examinations held in 2022 belong to the 2021 cycle.
- Shift is null for the two CPO 2019 questions and JHT 2020 question because the source does not give a shift. No shift was invented.
- Source records use SSC CGL, CHSL, CPO, Stenographer and JHT papers. All are within the SSC exam family.
- Each individual source URL is stored with its question and visible in admin and answer review.
- The teacher should check accepted-option nuances, particularly adverb placement (APV-001) and the omitted agent in APV-020, against the paper's final answer key if available.

This approved bank is seeded automatically on an empty database and opens the diagnostic. New unverified imports remain unpublished. The admin review/publish action is available for future candidate banks.
